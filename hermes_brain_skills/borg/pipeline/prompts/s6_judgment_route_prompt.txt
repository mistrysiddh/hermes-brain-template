# S6 Tacit — Judgment Route Sub-Prompt
# Per mem-draft-spec.txt §4 (Pipeline S6): Tacit — Judgment and nuance
#
# This sub-prompt governs routing a decision based on context — who should
# make it, how much deliberation is needed, what process to follow.

TASK: Route a decision based on context

INPUT:
- situation: description of the situation
- decision_type: type of decision (strategic, tactical, operational, personnel, technical)
- impact_level: potential impact (low, medium, high, critical)
- reversibility: how reversible is the decision (fully, partially, not)
- time_pressure: how much time is available (plenty, moderate, urgent, immediate)
- available_expertise: who has relevant expertise

STEPS:
1. Assess decision complexity based on type, impact, reversibility, time pressure
2. Determine who should make the decision (individual, team, escalate)
3. Determine deliberation process (quick, structured, formal, consensus)
4. Identify who needs to be consulted vs informed
5. Recommend decision-making approach (intuition, analysis, hybrid)
6. Identify escalation triggers

OUTPUT SCHEMA:
{
  "routing": {
    "decision_owner": "<individual|team|escalate>",
    "deliberation_process": "<quick|structured|formal|consensus>",
    "consulted": [<list of roles to consult>],
    "informed": [<list of roles to inform>],
    "approach": "<intuition|analysis|hybrid>",
    "complexity_assessment": "<low|medium|high|critical>",
    "escalation_triggers": [<list of conditions that warrant escalation>],
    "reversibility_check": "<decision is fully/partially/not reversible>",
    "time_sensitivity": "<plenty|moderate|urgent|immediate>"
  },
  "ok": <bool>,
  "errors": [<list of error strings, empty if ok>]
}

RULES:
- High impact + low reversibility = formal process, escalate
- High impact + high reversibility = structured process, team decision
- Low impact = quick process, individual decision
- Time pressure compresses the process
