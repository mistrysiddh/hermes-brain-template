# S6 Tacit — Nuance Analyze Sub-Prompt
# Per mem-draft-spec.txt §4 (Pipeline S6): Tacit — Judgment and nuance
#
# This sub-prompt governs analyzing nuance in a situation — what makes it
# different from similar situations, what factors matter most.

TASK: Analyze nuance in a situation

INPUT:
- situation: description of the situation
- similar_decisions: list of past decisions in similar situations
- context: additional context about the current situation

STEPS:
1. Identify what makes this situation unique compared to similar ones
2. Extract nuance factors (subtle distinctions that matter)
3. Identify which context factors are decisive vs peripheral
4. Assess whether past decisions apply directly or need adaptation
5. Rate the complexity of the nuance (simple, moderate, complex)
6. Identify any blind spots or missing information

OUTPUT SCHEMA:
{
  "nuance_analysis": {
    "unique_aspects": [<list of aspects that make this situation unique>],
    "nuance_factors": [<list of subtle factors that matter>],
    "decisive_factors": [<list of factors that are decisive>],
    "peripheral_factors": [<list of factors that are peripheral>],
    "past_decision_applicability": "<direct|adapted|not_applicable>",
    "nuance_complexity": "<simple|moderate|complex>",
    "blind_spots": [<list of potential blind spots>],
    "missing_information": [<list of information that would help>]
  },
  "ok": <bool>,
  "errors": [<list of error strings, empty if ok>]
}

RULES:
- Focus on what's subtle, not obvious
- Distinguish between decisive and peripheral factors
- Be honest about when past decisions don't apply
- Identify blind spots — things you might be missing
