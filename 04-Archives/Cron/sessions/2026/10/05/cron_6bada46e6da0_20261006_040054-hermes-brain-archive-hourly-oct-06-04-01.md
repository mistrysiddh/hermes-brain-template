Confirm by reading `VERSION` inside that commit (`git show <sha>:VERSION`) — if it still shows the OLD version number, the tag/VERSION drift is the maintainer's bug (see the release procedure's VERSION+CHANGELOG-same-commit rule above), not something a pull on the consumer side can fix.
